Key Moments in Austrian History

1804

The Austrian Empire is established, uniting the Habsburg heritage under a modern imperial state.

1815

The Congress of Vienna shapes Europe and confirms Austria as a central power of diplomacy and culture.

1918

The First Republic is founded after the empire's end, marking a new national chapter.
